Delete your Polyglot account

Polyglot (by Yurii Babii, developer “gemplay”) makes an account for you on first launch so your progress can be backed up. You can delete it, and everything stored with it, at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open Polyglot → Me → Settings (top right) → Delete account → Delete for good. The account and its cloud data are deleted at once. On iPhone this also revokes Sign in with Apple; on Android it removes Polyglot's access to your Google Account.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email gemplay.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Polyglot account” and the Apple ID or Google Account email you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done. An account that never signed in has no name or email, so it can only be deleted from the app; it is removed automatically after 180 days without use.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted: your account ID, the email and name Google shared (if you signed in with Google), and your learning progress in the cloud. Analytics and crash reports linked to the account are deleted on request and otherwise expire within 12 months. Purchase records may be kept where tax or accounting law requires it.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel Polyglot Max in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
