Privacy Policy

Polyglot — learn French through words you already know. Effective October 5, 2026.

In short

Polyglot keeps your learning progress on your phone. We have no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. The only outside services are Apple, which handles payments, and the few providers listed below that make subscriptions and, later, accounts and sync work.

Who we are

Polyglot is made by Yurii Babii, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: gemplay.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress is stored on your phone: the words and lessons you've done, streak and energy, your answers in onboarding (such as your goal, level and the language you learn through — English or Ukrainian), reminder time and settings. It is not sent to us.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the phone itself, and only if you allow notifications. There are no server push notifications. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Pronunciation

French words are played from recordings shipped with the app or spoken by the iPhone's built-in voice. Polyglot does not use the microphone and does not record you.

Purchases (Polyglot Max)

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card or billing details. Apple's handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

To check and restore your subscription we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). It receives the App Store purchase receipt and transaction history, a random app user ID that is not linked to your name, and basic technical data such as app version, iOS version, device model, country and IP address. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf and does not use it for advertising.

Accounts and sync

When accounts and sync arrive in Polyglot, this section applies:

  • Anonymous session. The app creates a random account ID so your progress can be backed up and synced, without asking for your name or email.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional. Apple gives us a stable user ID and, if you choose to share it, your email (which can be Apple's private relay address) and name.
  • Cloud sync. Your learning progress and your account ID are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), our database and sign-in provider, so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • Delete account. You can delete your account in the app. This removes your account and the progress stored in the cloud.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or data brokers; we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it except with the providers named above to run the app.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Why we use data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app and your subscription — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud — our legitimate interests.
  • To send reminders — your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings.
  • To keep records required by law — legal obligation.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you delete the app. Cloud data is kept while your account exists and is deleted within 30 days after you delete the account. Purchase records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires it.

Where data is processed

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where data leaves the EU/EEA, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Write to gemplay.app@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority — in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. Once accounts are available, “Delete account” in the app also removes your cloud data. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children and teens

Polyglot is meant for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live (up to 16 in some EU countries), use Polyglot with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to our providers travels over encrypted connections, and we limit what we collect to what the app needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above; important changes will also be shown in the app. Questions: gemplay.app@gmail.com.
