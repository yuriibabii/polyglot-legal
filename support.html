Common questions

How do I cancel Polyglot Max? iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Polyglot. Max stays active until the end of the paid period.

I paid but don't have Max. Open the paywall and tap “Restore purchases” while signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Polyglot in iOS Settings.
